### 4. Goods & Services (the most important section)

NabuLearn spans **software (Class 9)**, **education services (Class 41)**, and **SaaS (Class 42)**. File in all three for full protection. Each class = separate $350 fee (TEAS Standard) or $250 (TEAS Plus, if you use exact ID Manual wording).

**Class 9 — Downloadable & recorded software:**
> "Downloadable educational software for K-12 literacy instruction, reading assessment, phoneme practice, vocabulary building, and gamified learning; downloadable mobile applications for parent-teacher communication, student progress tracking, and emergency student safety notifications; downloadable software using artificial intelligence and machine learning for adaptive literacy assessment and personalized reading instruction."

**Class 41 — Education services:**
> "Educational services, namely, providing online non-downloadable instructional materials in the field of K-12 literacy, reading fluency, phonemic awareness, and vocabulary; providing an interactive website featuring educational games for children in the field of reading and literacy; educational assessment services, namely, evaluating student reading proficiency and literacy progress."

**Class 42 — SaaS / technology:**
> "Software as a service (SaaS) featuring software for K-12 literacy instruction, reading assessment, classroom management, gradebook administration, and student safety verification; providing temporary use of online non-downloadable software using artificial intelligence for adaptive literacy assessment and personalized learning; platform as a service (PaaS) featuring software platforms for school district administration, parent-teacher communication, and student progress analytics."

> **Tip:** Use the USPTO **ID Manual** (idm-tmng.uspto.gov) to copy exact pre-approved wording — qualifies you for cheaper TEAS Plus filing ($250/class instead of $350).
